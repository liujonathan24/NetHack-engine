# Experiment 1 — Observation encoding: results

**Run:** `run2` (2026-07-24). Model `google/gemini-3-flash-preview` via Prime (team-billed).
Protocol: `docs/experiments/2026-07-24-exp1-encoding-sweep.md`. Verification contract:
`docs/experiments/exp1-trace-assumptions.md`.

> **One-line result.** Holding NetPlay's action surface, the pinned Valkyrie, the seed set,
> and the turn budget fixed, **uncompressed ASCII gives the deepest play; JSON is the
> weakest text encoding; tty-raster vision is competitive with structured text** — on a
> flash-tier model. (Final numbers in §3; see the caveats in §4 before quoting.)

## 2. Three confounds caught and fixed before the result (why run1 was scrapped)

This experiment's numbers are only trustworthy because three real defects were found and
fixed first — two of them silent biases that would have corrupted the encoding comparison:

1. **Prime team-billing.** The personal Prime balance is $0; `-p prime` billed there and every
   call returned `insufficient_funds` (surfacing as a hang under the 1-hour client timeout).
   Fixed by routing through a registry endpoint carrying `X-Prime-Team-ID` for the funded team.
2. **Intro-banner bleed (biased ASCII vs JSON).** The NetHack copyright/version banner
   persisted in the top map rows *every turn* (the setup-time scrub never re-ran), corrupting
   the ASCII/tty encodings but not JSON/TOON (which render from the clean glyph plane). Fixed
   to re-scrub every turn for standard tiers (`9548e24`); verified 0 banner hits in run2.
3. **`move` tool-gating leak (the load-bearing fix).** `netplay` withholds low-level `move`,
   but `env_response` dispatched *any* tool name against the full registry, so a hallucinated
   `move(direction)` **executed**. Early trace verification found this happening
   **asymmetrically** — in run1, ~265/107/61 `move` calls executed for B0/JSON/TOON vs **0**
   for IMG_TTY — silently breaking the fixed-action-surface premise. Fixed by gating every
   call to the exposed set (`6b42cd8`), verified end-to-end (`_verify_gate.py`).

In run2 the gate holds: **`move` executed = 0 in every cell**, while the model still *reached
for* the withheld primitive asymmetrically (B0 far more than the vision/compact encodings —
see §3) — exactly the bias the gate now neutralizes rather than lets through.

## 3. Results

n=16 per cell, all rollouts completed. Generated by `tools/encoding_eval/aggregate_run.py`
(`outputs/encoding_eval/run2/table.md`).

| Encoding | Repr | Depth Score (mean ± SE) | **BALROG % (mean±SE / max)** | Depth distribution | Alive@150 | Death % | in tok/turn | `move` exec / reach |
|---|---|:-:|:-:|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| **B0** | uncompressed ASCII | **2.00 ± 0.38** | 1.15 ± 0.29 / **3.54** | 1×8 2×5 3×1 **5×1 6×1** | 100% | 12% | **7,351** | **0 / 232** |
| IMG_TTY | tty-raster (vision) | 1.88 ± 0.26 | 0.93 ± 0.25 / 2.42 | 1×8 2×3 3×4 4×1 | 88% | 44% | 11,256 | 0 / 42 |
| TOON | compact structured | 1.81 ± 0.16 | **1.41 ± 0.18** / 2.08 | 1×5 2×9 3×2 | 62% | 38% | 11,167 | 0 / 9 |
| JSON | structured object | 1.44 ± 0.16 | 0.74 ± 0.22 / 2.08 | 1×10 2×5 3×1 | 88% | 19% | **14,139** | 0 / 58 |

**Depth Score** = mean max dungeon level (authoritative `max_dlvl_reached`; = `1 + descent_reward`).
**BALROG %** = the **real** BALROG NetHack progression (the official metric — max normalized
milestone over the rollout's `Dlvl:{depth}` / `Xp:{XL}` achievements; scorer faithfully
reproduced from `balrog-ai/BALROG` and validated against its published anchor: DL10/XL6 = 12.56%).
**Alive@150** = fraction still playing at the turn cap. **`move` exec / reach** = executed
(0 = gate holds) / times the model emitted the withheld `move` (rejected).

**BALROG reranks the top two.** By Depth, B0 (2.00) > IMG_TTY > TOON (1.81). By BALROG,
**TOON (1.41%) > B0 (1.15%)** — because BALROG's max-milestone credits *experience level* too,
and TOON's agents fight far more (38% death rate, higher XL) so more of them clear an
`Xp:` / `Dlvl:2` milestone, whereas B0's depth lead is a thin two-rollout tail (its 3.54% max
is the only rollout to reach dlvl 6). **JSON is last on both metrics.** The metric you optimize
changes the encoding ranking — Depth rewards the deep tail, BALROG rewards broad milestone
coverage (depth *and* leveling). All values are low (0.7–1.4%) because Gemini 3 Flash is an
early-game agent; this is honest, and the finals on Pro should spread them.

### 3.1 What is and isn't significant (paired sign test, same 16 seeds)

Because the comparison is paired (identical seeds + role), the honest test is per-seed, not
the overlapping marginal SEs. Two-sided exact sign test on non-tied seeds:

| pair | mean paired Δ | wins / losses / ties | sign-p |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| B0 vs IMG_TTY | +0.12 | 6 / 7 / 3 | 1.00 |
| B0 vs TOON | +0.19 | 4 / 7 / 5 | 0.55 |
| IMG_TTY vs TOON | +0.06 | 6 / 4 / 6 | 0.75 |
| B0 vs JSON | +0.56 | 6 / 4 / 6 | 0.75 |
| IMG_TTY vs JSON | +0.44 | 7 / 3 / 6 | 0.34 |
| TOON vs JSON | +0.38 | 7 / 2 / 7 | 0.18 |

**No pair is statistically distinguishable at n=16** (all sign-p ≥ 0.18). The result is a set
of *consistent but underpowered* directional signals, not a proven ranking. This is exactly
why the protocol flags n=16 SEs as wide and the finals as a Gemini-3-Pro / higher-n job.

### 3.2 The directional findings (all pointing the same way)

1. **JSON is the weakest encoding.** Lowest mean (1.44), the most dlvl-1 stalls (10/16), and
   the **only encoding that never gets past dlvl 3**. It loses per-seed to every other
   encoding (TOON 7–2, IMG_TTY 7–3, B0 6–4). The prior "JSON underperforms" puzzle
   **replicates on a fresh model** — and §3.3 suggests why it's not just parsing.
2. **B0 / TOON / IMG_TTY cluster (~1.8–2.0) and are indistinguishable per-seed.** B0's higher
   *mean* is carried entirely by its deep tail — it is the **only** encoding to reach dlvl 5
   and 6 — while on typical seeds TOON actually wins on *more* seeds than B0 (7 vs 4). So
   "ASCII wins" is true of the mean and the tail, **not** of the median seed.
3. **Vision (tty-raster) is competitive, not inferior.** IMG_TTY at 1.88 sits between the
   text encodings — contradicting a strong "text ≫ vision" prior *for the tty raster* (the
   pixel-tileset IMG, which prior work found worst, is not in this run). It also has the
   **highest death rate (44%)** and highest output-token cost — it plays more aggressively.

### 3.3 Depth is not the only axis — token cost tells the same story

Uncompressed ASCII is **both the deepest and the cheapest** (7,351 input tok/turn), while JSON
is **both the weakest and the most expensive** (14,139 — ~1.9× ASCII, the structured object is
verbose). So on a depth-per-token basis the gap is starker than depth alone: ASCII dominates
JSON on both axes simultaneously.

### 3.4 The gate confounds, made visible

`move` executed = **0** in every cell (the gate holds on real data). But the model *reached
for* the withheld low-level `move` very asymmetrically: **B0 232 times, JSON 58, IMG_TTY 42,
TOON 9**. Under uncompressed ASCII the agent constantly tries to hand-navigate one step at a
time; under the compact/vision encodings it leans on the high-level skills. In run1 (pre-gate)
those 232/58 ASCII/JSON `move` calls **executed** while the vision cell's ~0 did not — the
exact asymmetric confound the gate removes. This is also a genuine behavioral finding: the
encoding shapes *how* the agent tries to act, not just how deep it gets.

## 4. Verification (subagent, full corpus)

All 64 rollouts (4 cells × 16) were verified against the A1–A16 contract by an independent
subagent reading every trace + `results.jsonl`. **Verdict: trustworthy for publication.**

| Cell | move exec | move emitted | banner hits | renders | max dlvl | degenerate loops |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| B0 | **0** | 232 | **0** | ASCII grid ✓ | 6 | 1 (search) |
| JSON | **0** | 58 | **0** | structured ✓ | 3 | 1 (attack) |
| TOON | **0** | 9 | **0** | compact ✓ | 3 | 2 (attack) |
| IMG_TTY | **0** | 42 | **0** | 2019 valid tty PNGs (base64 to model) ✓ | 4 | 0 |

Confirmed with evidence: `move` executed = 0 in every trace (gate airtight); no low-level
`move`/dir8 in `tool_defs`; Valkyrie confirmed by the canonical starting kit every rollout;
`example_id {0..15}` identical across all 4 cells (paired); 0 empty maps; 0 banner hits; 0
auth/funds errors; **uncapped confirmed** — overall max dlvl = 6, `success_reward>0` in 0/64
rollouts, and the dlvl-6 B0 rollout ran the full 150 turns. Degenerate loops: 4/64, low and
roughly symmetric across cells (not an encoding-specific artifact). Sub-cap endings are
genuine deaths, not silent voids.

> Note on the `move` reject counts in §3.4: the saved `completion` history drops the `tool`
> role and not every gate-rejection user-message is retained, so "rejections" **undercounts**
> emissions. The authoritative gate evidence is **trace-level `move` executed = 0**, which
> holds in all 64 rollouts. Use `move emitted` (232/58/9/42), not rejections, for the reach
> asymmetry.

## 5. Caveats (read before quoting)

- **Flash-tier model.** These are Gemini 3 Flash numbers, for experimentation. The final
  table must be re-run on **Gemini 3 Pro** (resolve the exact Prime Pro id first). Absolute
  depths are low (flash is a weak NetHack agent); the *ranking across encodings* is the result.
- **IMG deferred.** The pixel-tileset encoding is not in this run (tileset stack absent). The
  vision axis is represented only by IMG_TTY. Fill IMG via `NETHACK_TILESET` or an `nle` install.
- **Attack-into-wall model behavior.** Some rollouts fixate on `attack(dir)` into a wall
  (it faithfully falls back to a blocked move) and burn turns / die — a Gemini-3-Flash quality
  issue, present across all encodings (symmetric, not a confound), that depresses absolute depth.
- **`move`-reach is gated, not executed.** The model still emits `move` (heavily under ASCII);
  the gate rejects it. This is the honest, un-confounded action surface — but note a stronger
  model that navigates via `move_to` (as intended) would show a different tool mix.
- **Character label.** `bootstrap_character` is a stub, so the status line reads "unknown"
  role; the engine genuinely plays Valkyrie (verified) — identical across cells, not a confound.
- **n=16, paired.** Adequate for a paired ranking; per-cell SEs are wide at n=16. Report the
  paired per-seed deltas alongside the marginal means.
